Derek Okafor

I am a cluster reliability engineer based in Ashburn, Virginia. I grew up in Northern Virginia and never really left. I studied Network Engineering at Northern Virginia Community College. My first real job was NOC work at a regional ISP in Herndon. After that I moved to a colocation facility in Sterling, doing hands-on infrastructure. Eventually I ended up in Ashburn, working in data centers. On-call rotations, racking hardware, tracing cable paths, chasing down failures at 2am.

Right now I am on contract doing GPU cluster reliability. The work is distributed systems, networking, infrastructure, failure analysis and practical tooling. I keep clusters running. When a node drops out of a training job, I get paged. I know the fabric, the orchestration layer, the failure patterns.

At home I have four used A6000s in a closet. That is my homelab. I tinker when I can, which is not often. I have a two-year-old, and between the kid and on-call, the window for side projects is small.

Languages: English, Spanish, French, Arabic.

Skills and Tools: Linux, Kubernetes, Slurm, Python, Bash, Go, Ansible, Terraform, Prometheus, Grafana, DCGM, NCCL, InfiniBand, RDMA.
